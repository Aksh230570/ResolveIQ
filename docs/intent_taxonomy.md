# ResolveIQ Intent Taxonomy

This taxonomy is designed for AmazonHelp customer-support messages.

## 1. Delivery and Shipping

Questions or complaints about delayed, late, missing, or incorrectly estimated
deliveries.

Examples:

- Order has not arrived.
- Prime delivery is taking too long.
- Package says "arriving today" for several days.
- Courier marked the package incorrectly.

## 2. Order Tracking

Requests for tracking information or questions about shipment status.

Examples:

- Where is my order?
- Can I get the tracking number?
- Tracking information has not updated.

## 3. Wrong or Damaged Item

The customer received a wrong, damaged, defective, or incomplete product.

Examples:

- I ordered an iPhone but received another phone.
- The product arrived damaged.
- Something is missing from the package.

## 4. Returns, Refunds, and Cancellations

Requests or complaints involving returns, refunds, or order cancellation.

Examples:

- I want to return this item.
- My refund has not arrived.
- I need to cancel my order.

## 5. Payment and Billing

Problems involving payment, COD, charges, billing, or unexpected prices.

Examples:

- My payment failed.
- I was charged twice.
- My COD order has a problem.
- The price changed after ordering.

## 6. Account and Membership

Problems involving accounts, login, Prime membership, or account-related
benefits.

Examples:

- I cannot access my account.
- My Prime membership benefits are missing.
- I have a problem with my membership.

## 7. Website, App, or Technical Issue

Problems using Amazon's website, application, or digital services.

Examples:

- The website is down.
- The app is not working.
- I cannot complete an action on the website.

## 8. Product or Service Information

General questions about products, services, delivery policies, or Amazon
processes.

Examples:

- How does Prime delivery work?
- Can I order this product?
- What is the delivery policy?

## 9. Contact Support

Messages requesting a private message, direct contact, or connection with a
support representative.

Examples:

- Can I DM?
- How can I contact support?
- Please connect me to a representative.

## 10. Service Complaint or Negative Feedback

General dissatisfaction with Amazon's service, support, or experience where no
more specific intent is clear.

Examples:

- Your support keeps sending automated replies.
- This customer service is frustrating.
- Amazon's service is getting worse.

## 11. Other or Unclear

Messages that do not fit another category or do not contain enough information
to classify reliably.

## Annotation Rules

1. Assign exactly one primary intent.
2. Classify based on the customer's main request or problem.
3. Use the most specific applicable category.
4. Do not classify a message only from brand names or hashtags.
5. If multiple issues appear, choose the issue requiring the most direct support
   action.
6. Use `other_or_unclear` when the intent cannot be determined confidently.
